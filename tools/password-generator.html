Free Password Generator Online

Create strong and secure passwords instantly with our free Password Generator. Choose your password length and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Passwords are generated locally in your browser using secure cryptographic methods, so your password information never leaves your device.

Features

  • 100% Free
  • Cryptographically Secure
  • Custom Password Length
  • Uppercase and Lowercase Letters
  • Numbers and Symbols
  • Option to Exclude Look-Alike Characters
  • No Registration Required
  • Privacy Friendly

How to Generate a Strong Password

  1. Choose the password length using the length slider.
  2. Select uppercase and lowercase letters if required.
  3. Enable numbers and symbols for additional complexity.
  4. Optionally exclude look-alike characters.
  5. Click the Generate Password button.
  6. Copy your generated password and use it securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this password generator free?

Yes. Our Password Generator is completely free to use and does not require registration.

Are generated passwords stored or uploaded?

No. Passwords are generated directly in your browser and are not sent to or stored on our server.

How long can the password be?

You can choose a password length between 6 and 48 characters using the password length control.

Can I include numbers and symbols?

Yes. You can enable uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols according to your requirements.

Is the password generator secure?

The generator uses your browser's Web Crypto API to create random passwords locally, helping keep generated passwords on your device.
