Free BMI Calculator Online

Our free BMI Calculator helps you calculate your Body Mass Index instantly. Enter your height and weight to get your BMI result and understand the general BMI category associated with your result. The calculator supports both metric and imperial units and works directly in your browser.

How to Calculate BMI

  1. Select your preferred height unit.
  2. Enter your height in centimetres or feet and inches.
  3. Select your weight unit.
  4. Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
  5. Click the Calculate BMI button.
  6. Your BMI result and category will appear instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMI Calculator free?

Yes. Our BMI Calculator is completely free and does not require registration.

Does the BMI Calculator support pounds and feet?

Yes. You can calculate BMI using metric or imperial units, including kilograms, centimetres, pounds, feet and inches.

Are my height and weight uploaded?

No. The BMI calculation runs directly in your browser, so your entered information is not uploaded to a server.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measurement and should only be considered a rough guide rather than a medical diagnosis.
